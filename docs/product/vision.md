# Product Vision

## Purpose
- Provide a simple church information hub for Biserica Baptista Betel - Cluj-Napoca.
- Help church members quickly access upcoming events, important announcements, and frequently used links.
- Make access friction low through QR codes and direct links that work well on mobile devices.

## Outcomes
- Primary user outcome: members can find relevant church information in a few taps.
- Primary organizational outcome: church communication becomes more consistent and easier to maintain across sites.
- Success indicators: members successfully access event details, announcements, and curated links without needing separate manual guidance.

## Scope Direction
- In scope:
  - upcoming events
  - important information and announcements
  - bookmarked or curated links
  - filtering content by church site
  - an option to view all church-wide content
- Out of scope:
  - not defined yet
- Constraints already known:
  - the church operates as a multi-site organization
  - most users will likely arrive from QR codes or direct links
  - the experience should support both filtered and all-content views
